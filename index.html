Infinity

Unity 6 · URP · HLSL
Solo, AI-assisted · In development
~4 ms at 1080p

A space renderer where you don't render objects, you render spacetime. Every pixel is light integrated along a geodesic through a curvature field, so swapping the metric swaps the phenomenon.

Schwarzschild lensing runs as a fullscreen raymarch with a shadow radius matching theory to 0.05%. Hold SPACE and an Alcubierre bubble metric engages: the sky compresses into a forward cone, the rear goes black by infinite redshift.

Space Drone Simulator

Unity DOTS/ECS · Burst · Jobs
Solo · Public repository

Read the write-up →

The same 20,000-drone steering swarm implemented twice — ECS with Burst and Jobs, and idiomatic MonoBehaviours — sharing identical logic, so architecture is the only variable.

23 instrumented builds with median/p95/p99 sampling. The headline number is 21.7×, but the honest like-for-like number is 13.2× once you account for a collider flaw in the GameObject path.
